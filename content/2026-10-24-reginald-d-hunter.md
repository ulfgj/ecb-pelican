Reginald D Hunter is finally back in Brussels!

With his razor-sharp wit, fearless honesty and unmistakable Southern charm, Reginald D Hunter has become one of the most distinctive voices on the British comedy scene. Known for his brilliantly provocative takes on race, culture, politics and everyday absurdities, he combines intelligence with laid-back charisma in a way few comedians can match. A regular face on hit UK shows such as Live at the Apollo, Have I Got News for You and Never Mind the Buzzcocks, Hunter has spent more than two decades building a loyal international fanbase.

Now coming to Brussels, his latest live show promises an evening of bold, thought-provoking and outrageously funny stand-up from a true master of the craft — comedy that challenges, surprises and leaves audiences laughing long after the lights go down.
