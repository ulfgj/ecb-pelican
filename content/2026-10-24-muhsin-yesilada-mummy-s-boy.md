UK's new comedy sensation will perform in Brussels for the first time!

A BBC New Comedian of the Year nominee, Muhsin has quickly established himself as a standout live performer, regularly selling out solo shows across the UK. He has supported Mo Gilligan on tour, alongside acts such as Guz Khan, Kae Kurd, Babatunde Aleshe, Tez Ilyas, Nabil AbdulRashid and Richard Blackwood.

He was named a Komedia Top 10 Club Comedian and a Hot Water Comedy Club Breakthrough Act, marking him out as one of the most in-demand new-generation performers on the circuit.

Online, Muhsin has amassed over 100 million views, building a rapidly growing audience with his viral sketches and stand-up clips.

Special guest: Bianca Lala
