One of the UK’s biggest stand-up stars, Slim is a multi-award-winning comedian and a leading figure on the Black British comedy circuit. Renowned for his sharp observations, physical comedy and infectious energy, he has sold out major venues, including the London Palladium, becoming the first Black British comedian to do so with his own show.

His TV credits include Live at the Apollo (BBC Two), The Russell Howard Hour (Sky) and The Stand Up Sketch Show (ITV). Having performed worldwide and supported comedy giants Kevin Hart and Dave Chappelle, Slim brings over 20 years of experience and world-class comedy to Brussels.
