You’re mad about someone, you text them, they see that text … but they don’t text back!

“Waiting for Texto” is a hilarious comedy about dating in the age of social media. Aideen plays Lucy, unlucky in love, who spends an evening waiting for a text from her elusive love interest, Tom. The play is set in real-time and takes the audience on a rollercoaster of emotions, blending fast-paced monologues, punchlines, and relatable themes like social media’s vacuity and the struggles of modern communication. Aideen’s performance is both hilarious and bittersweet, delivering laughs while exploring relevant issues. “Bridget Jones meets Baby Reindeer, a must-see a with a feel good ending!
